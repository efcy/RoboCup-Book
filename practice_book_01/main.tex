\chapter*{How to use this book}
\markboth{How to use this book}{How to use this book}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to use this book}

This is the practical companion of \emph{Road to RoboCup}. That book explains the concepts (mathematics, neural networks, control, the rules of the Humanoid Soccer League and reinforcement learning with mjlab). This book has one goal: to get the \textbf{Booster K1} to work effectively, and you build it by \textbf{following along}.

\textbf{Structure.} The book is organised as a sequence of sessions at the laptop and at the robot. Every chapter has the same shape: \emph{what you need}, numbered \emph{steps}, a \emph{check} that tells you whether the step worked, \emph{if it does not work} (typical errors) and a short \emph{what to remember}. There are no practice problems; the checks take their place. Chapters that are marked \emph{Planned content} are not written yet.
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,label=\textbf{Part~\Roman*:},leftmargin=4.2em]
  \item \textbf{Setup:} your workstation and the simulator.
  \item \textbf{The robot:} unboxing, connection, the model.
  \item \textbf{Motions:} standing, walking and your own motions.
  \item \textbf{Deployment:} from simulation to the real robot, match day.
  \item \textbf{Bonus:} video analysis.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Boxes.} A \emph{Key idea} box (blue) contains the rule to remember. An orange \emph{step} box contains an action to carry out.

\textbf{Safety first.} A humanoid robot falls, pinches fingers and has strong motors. Read the safety rules of the first robot chapter before you switch on anything.
